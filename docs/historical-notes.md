# Historical Notes

## Annuario Pontificio alignment

The dataset follows the Annuario Pontificio-style distinction between canonical popes and those explicitly indicated as antipopes.

## Antipopes

Antipopes are separated into `data/antipopes.json` rather than mixed into the canonical list.

## Benedict IX

Benedict IX is modeled as one person with three separate pontificate records:

1. 1032-1044
2. 1045
3. 1047-1048

## Benedict X

Benedict X is treated as an antipope and excluded from canonical pope records.

## Pope-elect Stephen

Pope-elect Stephen is not modeled as a canonical pope. His removal from the official list affects the numbering of later Stephens.

## Numbering anomalies

Important numbering issues include Alexander, Benedict, Boniface, Felix, John, Martin/Marinus, and Stephen.
